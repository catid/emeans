# Phase 2A Classical Algorithmic Falsification Report (`H-Algo-1` through `H-Algo-5`)

All metrics are exact double-precision (`FP64`) full-dataset train and out-of-sample test MSE across **`15` paired random seeds per cell** (`3,600` runs total = `12` cells $\times$ `15` seeds $\times$ `20` arms; `528` pre-registered paired statistical comparisons; `0` non-finite values).
Negative `Delta %` indicates lower MSE (improvement over baseline); `p_wilcox` (`p_wilcoxon`) and `p_ttest` are two-sided paired tests.

## 1. Campaign Provenance & Experimental Controls

- **Cell-Intact Hardware Sharding**: Cells were sharded intact across 4 NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell GPUs (`shard_00`: `foureyes:GPU1` [`cifar10_r18_512_t2`, `multiscale_rare_t2`, `zipf_gmm_t2`]; `shard_01`: `loki:GPU0` [`cifar10_r18_512_t5`, `multiscale_rare_t5`, `zipf_gmm_t5`]; `shard_02`: `cashew.stratabaremetal.com:GPU0` [`cifar10_patches_128_t2`, `low_nk32_gmm_t3`, `stretched_a2_t2`]; `shard_03`: `acorn.stratabaremetal.com:GPU1` [`cifar10_patches_128_t5`, `isotropic_ctrl_t5`, `stretched_a2_t5`]). Within every cell, all `20` arms $\times$ `15` seeds ran sequentially on the **exact same physical GPU** after untimed global + per-cell Triton/cuBLAS warmup.
- **Exact Seed & Seeding Parity**: Within each `(cell, seed)`, all `11` `emeans_pp` arms shared identical `seed_emeans_plusplus` initial centroids and priors (`prior_mass_scale=0.5`), and all `9` `emeans` arms shared identical random-sample initial centroids.
